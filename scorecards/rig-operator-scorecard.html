Rules
  • Score evidence, not likeability. A likeable candidate is not automatically an 8.
  • Mandatory gates override the number. Gate failed means do not progress, whatever the score.
  • Safety concerns on trial override the number.
  • Scores evolve. Observed behaviour on trial carries more weight than anything written.
  • No extra weighting inside categories. Every criterion counts equally within its category.
